About

Having an interdisciplinary background allowed me to explore various topics, ultimately leading me to a passion for science communication — particularly in urban planning, sustainable development, and space science.

My interest in urban planning began in high school, inspired by Jane Jacobs, and grew as I studied how cities intersect with issues like sustainability, health, and safety. Exposure to the world of astrophysics sparked my love for space science and a desire to communicate complex ideas to broader audiences.
